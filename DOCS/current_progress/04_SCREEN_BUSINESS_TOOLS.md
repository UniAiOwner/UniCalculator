# 04. Screen: Business & Retail Tools

## 🎯 Purpose & Utility
The **Business Tools** module equips retailers, wholesalers, and small business owners with instant commercial decision-making calculators without complex spreadsheet setups.

## 🛠️ Available Tool Solvers

### 1. Margin & Markup Solver
- **Cost Price (CP)** vs **Selling Price (SP)** analysis.
- **Gross Profit**: Computes exact rupee profit (`SP − CP`).
- **Profit Margin (on SP)**: `(Profit / SP) × 100` (Emerald Green).
- **Markup (on CP)**: `(Profit / CP) × 100` (Amber Orange).

### 2. Discount & BOGO Evaluator
- Single and multi-tier progressive discounts (e.g. *Flat 20% + Extra 10%*).
- Calculates final payable price and actual net savings percentage.

### 3. Loan & Working Capital EMI Solver
- Computes monthly installment, total interest payable, and amortization summary for inventory or business loans.
