# 🔮 22. ITERATION ROADMAP & FUTURE HORIZONS
**Project**: UniCalculator (Bharat Pro Financial & GST Neumorphic Calculator)

---

## 1. Post-Launch Feature Horizons

### Phase 5.0 (Future Iteration): AI-Powered Bharat Commerce
1. **On-Device OCR Invoice & Barcode Scanner**:
   - Point smartphone camera at physical invoices, receipts, or product barcodes to automatically extract MRP, calculate base price, and verify HSN code GST rate.
2. **Indian Multilingual Voice Calculator**:
   - Offline voice input powered by lightweight on-device speech models (*"पंद्रह सौ में अठारह परसेंट जोड़ो"* ➔ Automatically evaluates `1500 + 18% = ₹ 1,770`).
3. **Multi-Business Multi-Counter Ledger**:
   - Support for managing multiple store cash registers (e.g. Counter 1, Counter 2, Delivery Boy Bag) with consolidated end-of-day tallying.
4. **Export to Tally XML & ClearTax JSON**:
   - 1-Tap export of daily cash registers directly formatted for Tally Prime and Indian GST filing portals.
