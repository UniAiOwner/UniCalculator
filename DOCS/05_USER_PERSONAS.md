# 👥 05. USER PERSONAS & ARCHETYPES
**Project**: UniCalculator (Bharat Pro Financial & GST Neumorphic Calculator)

---

## 👤 Persona 1: Ramesh Gupta — The Kirana & Wholesale Store Owner
- **Age**: 48
- **Location**: Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh (Tier 2 City)
- **Occupation**: Owner of "Gupta General Stores"
- **Tech Literacy**: Moderate (Uses WhatsApp, UPI apps, YouTube; avoids complex ERPs)
- **Primary Goals**:
  1. Quick 5% and 12% GST calculation on bulk food grain sacks and packaged FMCG goods.
  2. Evening cash closing: tallying ₹500, ₹200, ₹100 note bundles and sending the summary to his brother on WhatsApp.
  3. Wants big, clear, tactile buttons that feel like his old physical desktop calculator.
- **Frustrations**:
  - Small, flat touch screen buttons that cause accidental typos.
  - Making mistakes when writing deposit amounts in words at the local SBI branch.
- **App Usage Scenario**: Uses the **Neumorphic Cash Tally** tab every evening at 9:30 PM, uses **GST Pro** throughout the day for bulk orders.

## 👤 Persona 2: Priya Sharma — The Chartered Accountant Intern
- **Age**: 24
- **Location**: Ahmedabad, Gujarat (Tier 1 Commercial Hub)
- **Occupation**: Tax Article / Junior Accountant at a CA Firm
- **Tech Literacy**: High (Uses Excel, Tally Prime, Cloud Accounting)
- **Primary Goals**:
  1. Instant Reverse GST extraction: given a client's total bill (MRP), extract the Net Base Amount, CGST, and SGST for filing GSTR-3B.
  2. Quick verification of Inter-State IGST vs Intra-State CGST/SGST amounts.
  3. An infinite calculation tape where past calculations can be reviewed, edited, or copied into Excel workbooks.
- **Frustrations**:
  - Inaccurate floating-point decimals in generic smartphone calculator apps.
  - Apps loaded with noisy banner ads that interrupt high-focus audit work.
- **App Usage Scenario**: Uses **GST Pro** with the **Dual Breakdown Card** open on her desk while cross-checking physical invoices against tally sheets.

## 👤 Persona 3: Vikram Mehta — The Electronics & Mobile Trader
- **Age**: 34
- **Location**: Gaffar Market, Karol Bagh, New Delhi
- **Occupation**: Mobile Accessories Wholesaler & Importer
- **Tech Literacy**: High (Smartphone power user, B2B WhatsApp groups)
- **Primary Goals**:
  1. Calculate Profit Margin vs Markup % on gadgets bought at bulk wholesale cost and sold to local shopkeepers.
  2. Apply cascading trade discounts (e.g. `MRP - 35% Wholesale Discount + 18% GST`).
  3. Send professional, formatted WhatsApp price quotes to retailer clients within 5 seconds.
- **Frustrations**:
  - Forgetting the mathematical difference between Margin on Selling Price and Markup on Cost Price.
- **App Usage Scenario**: Uses the **Business Tools (Margin & Discount Stack)** module to generate on-the-fly quotes for WhatsApp B2B buyer inquiries.
