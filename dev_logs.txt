## 📅 Date: 2026-08-24 — Tab Scrubber Instant Navigation (Bottom Bar Decoupled)

### 🚀 Major Achievements:
- **Implemented "Tab Scrubber" / "Drag-to-Select Navigator" Pattern**:
  - **Root Cause Fixed**: `onFractionalDrag` was driving `pagerState.scrollToPage(targetPage, offset)` which physically scrolled the screen content during drag — causing app to look like it was minimizing or sliding.
  - **Fix #1 (`UniCalculatorApp.kt`)**: `fractionalPosition = null` + `onFractionalDrag = null` → Screen content NEVER moves during bottom bar drag. Pill manages its own internal drag state independently.
  - **Fix #2 (`UniCalculatorApp.kt`)**: All `animateScrollToPage(tween(350-380ms))` → `scrollToPage()` (instant, zero-latency) — applies to drag-release, tap, and all cross-screen navigate-to-history calls.
  - **Fix #3 (`NeumorphicSlidingBottomBar.kt`)**: Pill animation `tween(380ms)` → `spring(DampingRatioMediumBouncy, StiffnessMedium)` for physical snap-to-slot tactile feel.
  - Removed 15 lines of redundant fractional haptic tracking from app level — bottom bar already handles haptic detents internally.
- **Result**: Bottom bar drag → only pill + icon highlight moves. Screen jumps INSTANTLY on finger lift. Haptic detent fires at each tab during scrub.
